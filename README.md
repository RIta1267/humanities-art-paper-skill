# humanities-art-paper-skill

A Codex skill for humanities, social-science, and arts paper writing.

The skill is designed for Chinese and bilingual academic work in fields such as musicology, art theory, art education, folklore, intangible cultural heritage, cultural studies, theatre/opera, dance, visual art, museum studies, and media studies.

## What It Helps With

- Turning a broad topic into a research question and paper argument.
- Building outlines for journal articles, theses, opening reports, and course papers.
- Writing literature reviews by debate, method, and research gap instead of author-by-author summaries.
- Handling fieldwork, interviews, archives, local gazetteers, images, scores, policies, and repertory materials responsibly.
- Revising Chinese academic prose for CSSCI-style humanities writing.
- Designing humanities-friendly figures and tables such as timelines, transmission genealogies, fieldwork maps, repertoire tables, actor-institution networks, and process diagrams.

## Skill Folder

The installable skill folder is:

```text
humanities-art-paper/
```

Use it by copying that folder into your Codex skills directory, or by installing from this GitHub repository once published.

## Example Prompt

```text
Use $humanities-art-paper to turn my topic on county-level folk song transmission into a CSSCI-style article outline, with a literature review plan and figure/table suggestions.
```

## Design Notes

This skill borrows mature skill patterns such as staged intake, research-spine diagnosis, task routing, terminology ledgers, evidence mapping, section blueprints, figure QA, and final integrity checks. It adapts those patterns for interpretive humanities and arts scholarship rather than STEM-style paper structures.
